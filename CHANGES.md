## Installing on the iPhone (about 5 minutes, Xcode on the Mac)

1. Open `EMC FINALE/Mamori/Mamori.xcodeproj` in Xcode.
2. Click the blue **Mamori** project at the top of the left sidebar.
3. Select target **Mamori** → **Signing & Capabilities** → tick "Automatically manage signing" → **Team:** your Personal Team (Apple ID).
4. Select target **MamoriWidgetExtension** → same → same Team.
5. Plug the iPhone in with a cable, unlock it, tap **Trust**. Choose it in the device menu at the top of Xcode.
6. Press **▶ Run** (⌘R).
7. First time only, on the iPhone:
   - Settings → Privacy & Security → **Developer Mode** → On (the phone restarts).
   - Settings → General → **VPN & Device Management** → your Apple ID → **Trust**.
   - Press ▶ Run again.
8. Allow notifications when the app asks.
9. Long-press the home screen → **+** → Mamori → add the small or medium widget. Add the lock-screen widget from the lock-screen editor.

### If something fails

| Error | Fix |
|---|---|
| "Bundle identifier is not available" | Change both IDs, e.g. `com.vasistha2.mamori` and `com.vasistha2.mamori.widget`. The widget ID must start with the app ID. |
| App Groups will not provision on the free account | Delete the App Groups capability on both targets. The widget then shows the seed state (always day 12) and won't react to the rain button. In the demo, show the widget first, then open the app for the rain alert (spec §6 fallback). |
| "Untrusted Developer" on the phone | Step 7. |
| Widget missing from the gallery | Run once, open the app once, then restart the phone. |

### Before stage (spec §11)

- The app expires 7 days after install. **Install again on 17 or 18 Nov.** Do not reinstall on the morning of the 19th.
- Triple-tap the title → **Reset demo** before walking up.
